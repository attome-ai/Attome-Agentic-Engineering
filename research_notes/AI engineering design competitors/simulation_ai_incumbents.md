# AI-Driven Simulation Capabilities of Incumbent CAE / Cloud Simulation Vendors (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-03. Scope: Ansys/Synopsys, Siemens Simcenter, Dassault SIMULIA/3DEXPERIENCE, SimScale, Luminary Cloud, Rescale, COMSOL, and NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo/Omniverse as an enabler. The budget was limited to about 20 searches and fetches, so some per-product questions remain open (see the Gaps sections). Labels used: **[SHIPPED]** means the vendor says it is available or GA. **[PREVIEW]** means early access, exploratory, or early customer evaluation. **[ANNOUNCED/UNCLEAR]** means the status is not stated. **[MARKETING]** means a vendor claim that has not been independently verified. Items marked "(background, not re-verified)" come from pre-2026 knowledge and were not confirmed with a 2026 source in this session.

## Q1. Ansys (part of Synopsys): SimAI, Discovery, Engineering Copilot / AnsysGPT, optiSLang, GeomAI

### Takeaway
Ansys has the broadest AI portfolio among incumbents. SimAI is a mature, geometry-based surrogate that is physics-agnostic, needs about 30-100 training sims, and gives a per-prediction confidence level. Engineering Copilot/AnsysGPT is a documentation and help assistant. Ansys's first agentic features, Mesh Agent and the Discovery Validation Agent, arrived in 2026 R1 and were still in preview. No Ansys product takes a natural-language product brief and returns an optimized, verified CAD design end to end. The pieces (GeomAI, SimAI, optiSLang, Discovery, Mechanical) exist but are joined by an engineer, not an agent.

### Cited Findings
**Release context**
- Ansys 2026 R1 launched March 11, 2026 at Synopsys Converge. It is the first joint Synopsys/Ansys release — [Synopsys PR via PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1-to-re-engineer-engineering-with-joint-solutions-and-ai-powered-products-302711215.html); [DE247](https://www.digitalengineering247.com/article/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1).

**SimAI (surrogate) [SHIPPED]**
- Inputs: the user uploads legacy simulation data plus geometry and picks outputs of interest. Data from any vendor's solver works. SimAI is physics-agnostic ("all physics… from fluid mechanics to electromagnetics"), covers steady-state and transient, and predicts across topology variations — [Ansys SimAI page](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai).
- Training: typically 30-100 simulation results for "sufficient accuracy". Training is tuned for about 2 days (configurable 1-5 days). 2026 R1 supports datasets larger than 15 TB. Each customer gets an isolated workspace, and models are trained from scratch per customer — [SimAI page](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai).
- Speed and accuracy: predictions take seconds, with a claimed 10-100x shorter design cycle. Accuracy is described as "comparable to full-fidelity simulations", but no benchmark numbers are published [MARKETING] — [SimAI page](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai).
- Trust: each prediction carries a confidence level that flags designs too far from the training data and prompts retraining. Each model gets an autogenerated evaluation report — [SimAI page](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai).
- Visualization: predictions are continuous 3D fields. 2026 R1 added a Build panel for model tracking and expanded post-processing — [SimAI page](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai).
- Optimization: 2026 R1 added non-parametric optimization with enforced axial symmetry. Design optimization and generative design are listed as use cases — [SimAI page](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai).
- Deployment: SimAI Premium (cloud SaaS, or customer-hosted on own Kubernetes/VPC) and SimAI Pro (new in 2026 R1; desktop, local training on workstation GPUs, component-level). API: PySimAI Python SDK. Pricing is not public (contact sales) — [SimAI page](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai); [Synopsys PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1-to-re-engineer-engineering-with-joint-solutions-and-ai-powered-products-302711215.html).

**optiSLang [SHIPPED]**
- 2026 R1 adds SimAI connectors in optiSLang covering the full flow: training-data generation, AI training, then optimization and design studies. optiSLang plus Discovery offers sensitivity analysis and "one-click optimization" before validating in Mechanical, Fluent, or Icepak. Item-level status is not labelled — [Synopsys PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1-to-re-engineer-engineering-with-joint-solutions-and-ai-powered-products-302711215.html).

**GeomAI [ANNOUNCED/UNCLEAR status]**
- Generative AI for conceptual geometry exploration that learns from reference designs, introduced in 2026 R1 — [Engineering.com](https://www.engineering.com/synopsys-releases-ansys-2026-r1-engineering-platform/); [Synopsys PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1-to-re-engineer-engineering-with-joint-solutions-and-ai-powered-products-302711215.html).

**Agentic features [PREVIEW]**
- Mesh Agent (in Mechanical) diagnoses meshing failures and suggests remediation steps. Status: "available for exploratory use." The Discovery Validation Agent flags setup problems in Discovery. Status: "now advancing through early customer evaluations." Synopsys calls these the portfolio's "first agentic capabilities" — [Synopsys PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1-to-re-engineer-engineering-with-joint-solutions-and-ai-powered-products-302711215.html); [DE247](https://www.digitalengineering247.com/article/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1).

**Engineering Copilot / AnsysGPT [SHIPPED]**
- Engineering Copilot launched in 2025 R2 as a "virtual AI assistant" in the product UI, offering "one-click access to over 50 years of simulation expertise" — [Synopsys investor news 2025 R2](https://investor.synopsys.com/news/news-details/2025/Ansys-2025-R2-Enables-Next-Level-Productivity-by-Leveraging-AI-Smart-Automation-and-Broader-On-Demand-Capabilities-Solutions/default.aspx).
- It is available in Mechanical, Discovery, Fluent, HFSS, AEDT, Scade One, Speos, Maxwell, optiSLang, and Lumerical. 2026 R1 added medini analyze, ModelCenter, and Rocky — [Ansys help 2025 R2 release notes](https://ansyshelp.ansys.com/public/Views/Secured/corp/v252/en/mech_releasenotes/ds_Copilot.html); [Synopsys PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1-to-re-engineer-engineering-with-joint-solutions-and-ai-powered-products-302711215.html).
- Licensing: Copilot has no extra cost, but it requires the AnsysGPT AI+ license. AnsysGPT's knowledge base covers Ansys websites, articles, 800+ courses, and the forum, and it can create and track support cases. It is built on OpenAI models via Azure — [Ansys help](https://ansyshelp.ansys.com/public/Views/Secured/corp/v252/en/mech_releasenotes/ds_Copilot.html); [Ansys blog](https://ansys.synopsys.com/blog/work-faster-smarter-with-ansys-engineering-copilot).
- A July 2026 partner webinar lists "What's Next in Agentic AI" as a topic, with no details — [RandSim webinar](https://resources.randsim.com/videos-and-webcasts/ai-in-simulation-using-copilot-and-ansysgpt-for-faster-engineering-workflows).

**TwinAI**
- 2026 R1 adds fusion modeling, a temporal fusion transformer, and a ROM wizard for real-time digital twins — [Synopsys PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-launches-ansys-2026-r1-to-re-engineer-engineering-with-joint-solutions-and-ai-powered-products-302711215.html).

**Discovery and accessibility**
- Discovery is included free in Ansys Student (educational use only). Recent pages show a license valid to 03/31/2027. Refine-mode limits are about 128K structural nodes/elements and 512K-1M fluid cells (sources conflict). It requires a recent GPU, and the full install is about 50 GB — [Ansys Student](https://www.ansys.com/en-gb/academic/students/ansys-student).
- NVIDIA Omniverse Blueprint: Ansys was the first adopter, applying it to Fluent — [IT Brief](https://itbrief.com.au/story/nvidia-unveils-omniverse-blueprint-for-digital-twins).

### Inferences
- Background, not re-verified: Discovery's "Live" GPU physics gives interactive structural, thermal, and fluid results while you edit geometry. Its Refine mode gives higher-fidelity solves. Discovery also includes topology optimization. This is Ansys's main real-time interactive offering.
- Ansys's assistant is grounded in its documentation and does not autonomously set up or run solves. The only setup-aware agents (Mesh Agent and Validation Agent) diagnose problems and are still in preview. On autonomy, Ansys trails SimScale.
- SimAI's need for 30-100 customer sims is a real adoption barrier for one-off designs. It suits families of similar parts, not a cold-start NL brief.
- Ansys has the strongest verification credentials in the field (solver pedigree, confidence score, evaluation reports), but at enterprise cost.

### Gaps
- No public information on Ansys 2026 R2 (normally due around July 2026) was found. Whether the Discovery Validation Agent reached GA is unknown.
- No published SimAI accuracy benchmarks or pricing. GeomAI's maturity (GA or beta) is unclear.
- Independent user reviews of SimAI or Copilot limitations were not found in this session.

## Q2. Siemens Simcenter (PhysicsAI, HEEDS, Simsolid/Designcenter, Industrial Copilot)

### Takeaway
Siemens's AI story in 2026 centres on Simcenter PhysicsAI, a geometric deep-learning surrogate. It is now embedded across the portfolio, including the absorbed Altair tools (Inspire, HyperMesh). PhysicsAI Generate, which creates concepts from targets and historical data, was newly introduced in July 2026. HEEDS remains the multi-objective optimizer. Copilots exist (Design Copilot NX, and a Simcenter agent in the Industrial Copilot family), but they are help or assistant tools. No source documents an autonomous simulation agent from Siemens. No product named "Simcenter X" was found.

### Cited Findings
- July 2026 Simcenter release: the portfolio is unified with Altair tools, with expanded AI-driven simulation and GPU acceleration — [Siemens PR via PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/siemens-accelerates-engineering-simulation-with-a-unified-ai-powered-simcenter-portfolio-302835381.html); [Siemens newsroom](https://news.siemens.com/en-us/siemens-simcenter-summer-2026/).
- Simcenter PhysicsAI: geometric deep learning that turns simulation data into predictive models. It claims "up to 1,000x faster than traditional solver simulations" [MARKETING]. It is now integrated into more products, including Simcenter Inspire (structural plus casting, molding, and extrusion forming) and Simcenter HyperMesh ("more efficient data training and greater confidence in results") — [Siemens PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/siemens-accelerates-engineering-simulation-with-a-unified-ai-powered-simcenter-portfolio-302835381.html).
- PhysicsAI Generate [newly introduced; GA status not labelled]: "physics-aware generative AI" that generates design concepts "in seconds" from target dimensions, performance requirements/KPIs, and historical engineering data — [Siemens PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/siemens-accelerates-engineering-simulation-with-a-unified-ai-powered-simcenter-portfolio-302835381.html).

## Q4. SimScale (cloud FEA/CFD/thermal, Physics AI, Engineering AI Agent)

### Takeaway
SimScale is the most aggressive incumbent on agentic simulation and the closest direct competitor to a natural-language-to-verified-design agent. Its Engineering AI Agent takes a single prompt, then chooses physics, prepares and cleans CAD, meshes, sets boundary conditions, runs solves, runs mesh-independence studies, and explains results. It is free on every plan (from autumn 2026), runs inside Onshape (Sept 2026), and is exposed through an API and MCP. What it does not clearly do is generate or optimize new geometry in a closed loop. Physics AI surrogates are a separate product.

### Cited Findings
- May 2026: an enterprise guided pilot service in which SimScale engineers deploy Engineering AI agents on live projects ("From Engineering Intent to Validated Design in Minutes") — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260507641171/en/From-Engineering-Intent-to-Validated-Design-in-Minutes-SimScale-Opens-Engineering-AI-Agents-to-All-Enterprise-Teams).
- August 2026: the agent was opened to the whole community of more than 900,000 engineers. The company says it can "run complete simulation workflows autonomously, from CAD geometry clean-up, to simulation setup and results" — [SimScale press](https://www.simscale.com/press/engineering-ai-agent-open-to-community/).
- September 15, 2026 (IMTS): Engineering AI Agent for Onshape, delivered through the Onshape App Store. From one prompt it proposes the setup, prepares CAD (including geometry that is not yet simulation-ready), selects physics, meshes, sets BCs, runs, and interprets results in plain language. It covers CFD, FEA, thermal, and EM. The engineer can override BCs or review assumptions at any point. It runs on the SimScale Agent API with auditable runs — [SimScale press](https://www.simscale.com/press/simscale-launches-engineering-ai-agent-for-onshape/).
- Autumn 2026 update:
  - The agent reads result files and extracts values (for example, maximum face temperature or peak pressure location) and can produce screenshots that match a reference image.
  - It adjusts mesh settings, creates refinement regions, starts, stops, and tracks meshing, and can run a mesh independence study and compile the results (a verification feature).
  - Users can run it asynchronously: they ask and go do something else while it works.
  - Multiple parallel agent conversations can run per project.
  - It is accessible through the API and MCP for agent-to-agent workflows.
  - It is free on every plan, and Community users get an AI credit allowance.
  - Source: [SimScale Autumn 2026](https://www.simscale.com/blog/whats-new-simscale-autumn-2026/).
- Geometry and optimization: the autumn notes do not say the agent modifies design geometry or runs optimization. CAD repair is described as manual tools. A customer quote mentions an AI model that "can generate a new optimized design in under an hour", but it is not tied to the agent — [SimScale Autumn 2026](https://www.simscale.com/blog/whats-new-simscale-autumn-2026/).
- Physics AI: users can train on simulation data from other tools (tessellation, surface or volume mesh, VTK family, MED, XDMF) through the UI or API, then predict inside SimScale — [SimScale Autumn 2026](https://www.simscale.com/blog/whats-new-simscale-autumn-2026/). Physics AI models were trained on thousands of high-fidelity CFD sims using NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo — [Design News](https://www.designnews.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-tools-for-engineers-simscale).
- Solvers and physics: fluid, structural, thermal, EM, and multiphysics. Marc thermo-mechanical with convection, radiation, and heat flux. Transient magnetics with SPICE circuit coupling. The Simerics-MP mesher and solver (turbomachinery, conjugate heat transfer) was added to the Community plan in June 2026. GPU solvers (including LBM) are excluded from Community — [SimScale Autumn 2026](https://www.simscale.com/blog/whats-new-simscale-autumn-2026/).
- Visualization (browser): custom out-of-range colours (for example, purple above yield), legend rescaling to visible or selected parts and cut planes — [SimScale Autumn 2026](https://www.simscale.com/blog/whats-new-simscale-autumn-2026/).
- Pricing: the free Community plan (public projects) has selected analysis types, 10 unrestricted simulations, and up to 3,000 core hours. Mechanical, Professional, and Enterprise are custom-quoted — [SimScale pricing](https://simscale.com/pricing/); [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/145960/SimScale/pricing).
- Positioning talk: "From Tools to Agents: agentic engineering workflows" at CDFAM — [CDFAM](https://cdfam.com/simscale-bcn/).

### Inferences
- SimScale already ships most of the "describe it and get it simulated" loop: prompt to setup to mesh to solve to explanation, plus mesh-independence verification. The gap left for a StructuraAI-style product is (a) NL-driven geometry generation or modification and (b) closed-loop optimization producing Pareto fronts and design comparison. Neither is shown as an agent capability.
- Background, not re-verified: SimScale has offered parametric studies and design-of-experiments style sweeps for some analysis types. It does not offer a native multi-objective optimizer that produces Pareto fronts.
- A free agent on a free plan with more than 900,000 users is a strong distribution advantage. Community projects are public, which limits proprietary use.

### Gaps
- The agent's accuracy or error rates, and any independent reviews, were not found. All sources are vendor PR or blogs.
- Physics AI training-sample needs, speed, and UQ figures are not published in the sources found.

## Q5. Luminary Cloud (GPU CFD, Physics AI, SHIFT models, Lumi AI)

### Takeaway
Luminary is a GPU-native, CFD-centric cloud platform that has pivoted into a "physics AI model factory". It ships a growing family of pre-trained SHIFT foundation and surrogate models for aero, defense, crash, battery thermal, and truck aero, along with tools to train custom models. Lumi AI is an assistant for meshing and setup (mesh adaptation, cost and runtime estimates), not a design agent. An MCP server and an in-app notebook AI assistant connect external coding agents. Structural capability comes mainly through the SHIFT-Crash surrogate, not a general FEA solver.

### Cited Findings

  - SHIFT-CCA (drone/collaborative combat aircraft), SHIFT-Submarine, and SHIFT-Pump: January 2026, defense-focused — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/12/3216955/0/en/luminary-launches-new-secure-advanced-physics-ai-models-to-power-u-s-department-of-war-by-air-land-and-sea.html); [Washington Technology](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/companies/2026/01/luminary-cloud-expands-physics-ai-toolkit-defense-focused-models/410679/).

## Q7. COMSOL Multiphysics (Chatbot, surrogate models)

### Takeaway
COMSOL 6.4 (current release, late 2025 into 2026) has an optional Chatbot window that combines COMSOL documentation with context from the active model. It can connect to GPT-5, DeepSeek, Gemini, or any OpenAI-API-compatible model. It is an assistant, not an autonomous agent. Surrogate model training (Gaussian process, and DNN since 6.2/6.3) is built in, with UQ, and is aimed at fast simulation apps. COMSOL has the strongest general multiphysics fidelity of the group but no geometry-generating AI.

### Cited Findings
- 6.4 Chatbot "now supports connections to GPT-5, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, and other OpenAI API-compatible models". Assistance combines "COMSOL documentation with information from an active simulation" — [COMSOL 6.4 release](https://comsol.com/release/6.4).
- Chatbot history: introduced in 6.3 as an optional window that helps with Java code for the COMSOL API and answers general questions (secondary source) — [SimTool](https://www.simtool.com/article/ai-powered-simulation-modeling-the-revolutionary-transformation-reshaping-engineering-in-2025/). ## Q8. NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo / Modulus / Omniverse (enabling platform)
